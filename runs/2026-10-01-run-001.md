## 1. SafeLine: back-feed interlock and digital permit-to-work for linemen ★

- **Challenge:** 03 Grid Reliability
- **Hook:** "Before a lineman touches a wire, the SCADA proves it is dead, including power coming back from rooftop solar, inverters and DG sets."
- **Problem:** Lineman electrocutions in India are frequent **[verify the stats]**. Many happen on lines thought to be isolated but back-fed by home inverters, DG sets or rooftop PV whose anti-islanding failed. Shutdown permits are paper or phone calls. As rooftop solar spreads, back-feed risk grows. Unsafe work also slows restoration, because crews wait for confirmation.
- **Mechanism (SCADA-native):**
  1. A digital permit-to-work request is raised from the lineman's phone for a segment on the single-line diagram.
  2. The SCADA opens the upstream breaker or recloser, then checks every source downstream on the SLD (rooftop inverters, battery inverters, DG sets on telemetry) for voltage or export.
  3. It commands registered inverters to trip or block export (Modbus/SunSpec) and verifies zero voltage.
  4. Permit issue needs double confirmation (Junior Engineer approves, lineman acknowledges). The segment is locked on the SLD, so no one can re-energise until the permit is returned.
  5. A full audit trail is kept.
- **Not a generic AI idea because:** it is interlocking, a core SCADA protection and control function. There's no chatbot and no forecasting dashboard.
- **scadaheck reuse:** ~75%. SLD, alarms, double confirmation, RBAC (lineman / JE / SDO / control room), Modbus simulator.
- **New work:** a permit-to-work state machine, a back-feed source registry on the SLD, a segment lock, and a mobile permit screen.
- **Impact metric:** back-feed exposure events (target: 0), permit turnaround time vs paper or phone, restoration time.
- **Business model:** DISCOM SaaS per feeder, aligned with RDSS distribution modernisation **[verify scheme fit]**. A rooftop-solar registry module could be required for net-metering approvals.
- **Schneider appeal:** high. Safety is a core Schneider value, and the idea extends ADMS/EcoStruxure Grid into low voltage.
- **Risks:** inverter remote control needs installer or vendor cooperation. Fallback is detection plus an alert-only mode.

## 2. LastGasp: fault location from smart-meter power-fail events

- **Challenge:** 03
- **Hook:** "250 million smart meters are being installed. Each one can shout 'I lost power'. We turn those shouts into a fault map."
- **Problem:** Rural and urban LV faults are found by linemen patrolling and by customer phone calls. That takes hours.
- **Mechanism:** Smart meters send last-gasp or power-fail events **[verify IS 16444 support]**. The SCADA overlays them on the feeder topology (SLD), finds the smallest upstream device common to all dark meters, flags the faulty segment, and suggests switching to restore the healthy sections (FLISR-lite).
- **Not generic because:** it does topology-based fault localisation, which is a classic SCADA/ADMS function, with no extra hardware.
- **Reuse:** ~65%.
- **New work:** a topology tree model, an event ingestion simulator, and a localisation algorithm.
- **Impact metric:** fault localisation time, SAIDI/CAIDI.
- **Business model:** a DISCOM module on top of existing smart-meter head-end systems.
- **Schneider appeal:** high (ADMS, FLISR).
- **Risks:** depends on the head-end system providing events. Pairs very naturally with #1.

## 3. ColdStart: managed restoration that prevents re-tripping

- **Challenge:** 03
- **Hook:** "When power returns after an outage, every fridge, AC and pump starts at once and the feeder trips again. We bring the neighbourhood back in waves."
- **Problem:** Cold-load pickup after long outages causes inrush and repeated trips, especially in summer.
- **Mechanism:** The SCADA sequences re-energisation section by section, using a community battery to support the inrush and smart-meter load limits to stagger high loads (AC, pumps) over 10–20 minutes.
- **Reuse:** ~60%.
- **Impact metric:** re-trip count after restoration, time to full restoration.
- **Schneider appeal:** medium-high.
- **Risks:** needs a credible inrush model in the simulator.

## 4. PhaseJuggler: low-voltage phase rebalancing to save distribution transformers

- **Challenge:** 03
- **Hook:** "India's distribution transformers burn out partly because one phase is overloaded while another sits idle."
- **Problem:** Single-phase connections are assigned haphazardly, so phases are unbalanced. That raises neutral current and losses and causes DT failures **[verify the Indian DT failure rate]**.
- **Mechanism:** Use smart-meter data plus the DT phase currents to infer each consumer's phase, then give linemen a minimum-move reconnection plan ("move these 7 houses from R to Y").
- **Not generic because:** it's an optimisation over the network model, with a work-order output.
- **Reuse:** ~50%.
- **Impact metric:** neutral current, DT loss, peak phase loading.
- **Business model:** DISCOM loss-reduction service. **Risks:** inferring phases from data is non-trivial.

## 5. Water Is the Battery: agricultural pump fleet as dispatchable load

- **Challenge:** 01 Agriculture
- **Hook:** "A village's water tanks and soil can store solar energy better than a lithium battery."
- **Problem:** Agricultural feeders supply pumps in fixed slots, often at night. Midday solar is wasted, and simultaneous pump starts cause voltage dips.
- **Mechanism:** The SCADA treats GSM-starter pumps as a fleet. It schedules them into the solar window, staggers starts to avoid voltage dips, and respects each farm's water need (crop × soil moisture).
- **Reuse:** ~55%.
- **Impact metric:** kWh shifted to solar hours, voltage-dip events, farmer pumping hours delivered.
- **Business model:** DISCOM / PM-KUSUM feeder-solarisation operations **[verify]**.
- **Risks:** a crowded theme (solar pumps). The differentiator is the fleet control.

## 6. Rickshaw Grid: e-rickshaw charging as feeder flexibility (lateral)

- **Challenge:** 03
- **Hook:** "Lakhs of e-rickshaws plug in every evening, often on informal connections, right at the feeder peak."
- **Mechanism:** Charging hubs are put under SCADA control. Charging moves to solar or off-peak windows, with a guaranteed state of charge by the morning shift and a price incentive.
- **Reuse:** ~55%.
- **Impact metric:** evening peak reduction, share of rickshaw charging from solar.
- **Business model:** hub operator plus DISCOM time-of-day tariff.
- **Risks:** driver behaviour, informal connections.

## 7. LeakHunter: compressed-air leak detection with zero new sensors

- **Challenge:** 04 Smart Manufacturing
- **Hook:** "Up to a quarter of an SME's compressed air leaks away **[verify]**. We find it from the compressor's own Modbus data during lunch break."
- **Mechanism:** The SCADA runs an automatic "no-demand test" when production is idle (shift change, lunch, Sunday). Compressor load and unload cycles with zero demand show leak flow. It trends the leak rate, estimates ₹ lost, and raises a maintenance work order.
- **Not generic because:** this is a physics test run from the existing controller, not AI.
- **Reuse:** ~50%.
- **Impact metric:** kWh per Nm³, leak CFM, ₹/month.
- **Business model:** low-cost subscription for MSME clusters.
- **Risks:** narrower scope, less "grid".

## 8. LiftSafe Society: DG-free power backup for housing societies

- **Challenge:** 02 Smart Buildings
- **Hook:** "When the grid fails, the first job is getting people out of the lift, not starting the diesel set."
- **Mechanism:** In an outage, the building SCADA (rooftop PV plus battery) runs a priority sequence: lift rescue to the nearest floor → water pumps → corridor and emergency lights → common areas. The DG set starts only if the forecast says the battery will run out.
- **Reuse:** ~60%.
- **Impact metric:** DG litres and hours avoided, lift entrapment incidents.
- **Risks:** overlaps with LifeLine. Lift integration is a safety-certified domain.

## 9. Honey-Grid: OT cyber decoy for DISCOM SCADA (lateral)

- **Challenge:** 03 (reliability through security)
- **Hook:** "Our Modbus simulator becomes a trap: a fake substation that catches attackers before they reach the real one."
- **Mechanism:** Deploy scadaheck's Modbus TCP simulator as high-fidelity decoy RTUs. Unexpected writes raise alarms with the attacker's fingerprint.
- **Reuse:** ~70%.
- **Impact metric:** detection time.
- **Risks:** a weak match to the energy-impact metrics the challenge asks for. Better as a supporting feature than a headline.

## 10. MeltWave: induction-furnace scheduler for foundry clusters (lateral)

- **Challenge:** 04
- **Hook:** "Foundries in Coimbatore, Rajkot and Kolhapur pay maximum-demand penalties because three furnaces melt at once."
- **Mechanism:** The SCADA staggers melt cycles across furnaces (and across a cluster) to stay under contract demand, aligns melts with time-of-day tariff windows, and pre-heats during solar hours.
- **Reuse:** ~50%.
- **Impact metric:** maximum-demand charges, specific energy consumption (kWh per tonne), tariff savings.
- **Business model:** foundry-cluster association subscription.
- **Risks:** a niche domain; needs real furnace profiles **[verify]**.
